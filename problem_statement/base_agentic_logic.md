
The proposed system is an **AI-driven Customer Support Automation platform** designed to autonomously handle common customer service requests through a collaborative multi-agent workflow. Customers interact with a conversational AI interface that understands natural language queries and identifies the user's intent. Based on the detected intent, the request is dynamically routed to specialized agents responsible for tasks such as refunds, replacements, order cancellations, shipping inquiries, billing support, complaint handling, technical assistance, and coupon generation. Each specialized agent performs the required business logic by interacting with enterprise tools and synthetic customer data to process requests efficiently.

For operations requiring managerial intervention, the workflow incorporates a **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL)** approval mechanism through a dedicated administrative dashboard. Requests exceeding predefined business thresholds or requiring manual verification are forwarded to an administrator for approval or rejection before execution. The system also provides real-time status updates and notifications through an integrated web interface, allowing both customers and administrators to monitor request progress without relying on external communication services.

The architecture follows a  **fan-out/fan-in multi-agent orchestration pattern** , where a central intent-routing agent distributes requests to domain-specific agents, and the processed results are consolidated before being returned to the user. The entire platform operates on synthetic enterprise data, enabling realistic simulation of customer support workflows while providing a safe and reproducible environment for experimentation, evaluation, and future integration of governance and security mechanisms.
